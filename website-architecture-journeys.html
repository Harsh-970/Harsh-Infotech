3. Strategic UX Bottleneck Audit

Auth Gate Drop-off High Risk

Intercepting immediate WhatsApp inquiries with a compulsory sign-up modal causes a potential 15–20% lead conversion drop-off.

💡 Recommendation: Log lead details non-intrusively in background storage.
Redundant Footer Links Medium

The global footer includes inactive anchors (e.g. Careers (#)) and repeats menu hierarchies already present in the sticky navbar.

💡 Recommendation: Clean up footer links to point to active pages only.
Light Theme Contrast Low

Under light mode, translucent card borders have an opacity of 0.45, which falls slightly below ideal WCAG accessibility guidelines.

💡 Recommendation: Increase border opacity to 0.6 in src/index.css.
